# Our Love Story

​Create an elegant, modern, and interactive digital wedding invitation web app (mobile-first approach) inspired by classic minimalism with a soft, romantic touch.

​1. Visual Style & Palette:

​Primary Color: Royal / Gradient Classic Blue (matching the monogram/logo color).

​Background: Clean White / Light Off-White background with subtle elegant shadows and smooth rounded cards.

​Typography: Elegant serif fonts for titles/headings and a crisp, readable sans-serif font for body text and buttons.

​Vibe: Romantic, sophisticated, clean, and interactive.

​2. Hero Section (Header):

​Display the couple's monogram logo prominently at the top (I & R with elegant calligraphy and date 26 / 09 / 2026).

​A warm welcoming subtitle: "Com a bênção de Deus e de nossos pais, convidamos você para celebrar o nosso casamento!"

​3. Couple's Photo Section:

​Add a dedicated placeholder image container for a high-quality portrait photo of the couple.

​Soft rounded corners or circular frame with subtle blue border glow or light shadow.

​4. Event Details & Interactive Countdown:

​Date: 26 de Setembro de 2026 (September 26, 2026).

​Interactive Countdown Timer: Live timer displaying Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds remaining until the wedding day.

​5. Interactive Buttons & Actions:

​Button 1: "Ver Localização no Mapa" (Primary CTA)

​Link directly to Google Maps: [https://maps.app.goo.gl/8fGcMvLjXubdZ1Dh8](https://maps.app.goo.gl/8fGcMvLjXubdZ1Dh8)

​Styled with a map pin icon, primary blue fill, and smooth hover effect. Opens in a new tab.

​Button 2: "Confirmar Presença (RSVP)"

​Opens a clean interactive modal form asking for:

​Name

​Will you attend? (Yes / No)

​Number of guests attending

​Dietary restrictions or special message (optional text area)

​Submit button with a success confirmation toast/message.

​Button 3: "Adicionar ao Calendário"

​Allows guests to easily add 26/09/2026 to Google Calendar or Apple Calendar.

​Button 4: "Lista de Presentes" (Optional/Modal)

​A section or modal with details for PIX key or external gift registry links.

​6. UX/UI Features:

​Fully responsive, mobile-optimized layout (most guests will open this on smartphones).

​Subtle animations (fade-in on scroll, smooth button hovers, heart icon animations).

​Add a toggleable ambient background music player button (e.g., romantic instrumental music option).
